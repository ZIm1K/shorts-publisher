Shorts Publisher

Privacy policy

Effective 24 November 2026.

Shorts Publisher is a private desktop tool. It runs on its developer's own computer and connects only to YouTube channels the developer owns. It has no hosted service and no other users. This policy describes what the tool does with Google account data.

Data the tool accesses

With the permission granted on Google's consent screen, the tool uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos, titles and descriptions to the connected channel;
  • read the connected channel's identifier, name and list of uploads;
  • read statistics and YouTube Analytics reports of that channel's videos.

It does not read email, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used and stored

  • The access tokens issued by Google and the statistics of the uploaded videos are stored only on the computer where the tool runs.
  • The data is used only to publish the videos and to plan future ones.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time on the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. After that the tool can no longer reach the account, and the tokens stored on the computer stop working and can be deleted.

Third-party terms

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Their use is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
