Shorts Publisher

A small desktop tool that uploads short videos to YouTube on a schedule and reads back their public statistics.

What it does

  • Uploads finished short videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the YouTube channels of the person running the tool.
  • Reads the basic details of those channels to make sure a video goes to the right one.
  • Reads view counts and analytics of the uploaded videos to plan the next ones.

Who can use it

Shorts Publisher is not a public service. It runs on its developer's own computer and is connected only to YouTube channels the developer owns. There is no sign-up, no hosted version and no other users.

Google account access

The tool asks for permission to upload videos, to view the YouTube account, and to view YouTube Analytics reports. It uses YouTube API Services, so using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. What happens with the data is described in the privacy policy.
